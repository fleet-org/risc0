# Workarounds, stubs and shortcuts — the register for expert review

Everything here unblocked the goal without a formal justification, or with a justification that a
reviewer may not accept. Each entry says what was done, where, what it unblocked, what the formal
solution would be, and the risk. Claims built on an entry carry the tag WORKAROUND next to MEASURED
/ INFERRED / UNVERIFIED. The review column is the expert's.
